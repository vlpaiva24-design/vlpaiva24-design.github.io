OpenClaw Drive Agent is a personal assistant operated by Vladimir Pak. Through a Telegram conversation, it helps the account owner find, read, create, and edit files in Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, and Slides.

How it works

The owner connects their Google account using OAuth and sends an instruction to the assistant. The assistant uses the granted Google access to carry out that instruction. It does not offer public sign-up or access to other people's files.

Google access

The assistant requests access to Google Drive and supported editors so it can search files, read content, create documents, and update existing files when the owner asks. Google access can be revoked at any time in Google Account connections settings.

Privacy and support

Read the privacy policy for details on data handling, third-party services, and how to withdraw access. For questions, contact pakvd88@gmail.com.
