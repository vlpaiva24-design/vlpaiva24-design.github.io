Privacy policy

This policy describes how OpenClaw Drive Agent, a private assistant operated by Vladimir Pak, handles information from the connected Google account.

Information accessed

With the account owner's authorization, the assistant can access files and file metadata in Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, and Slides. It may read, create, or edit files as needed to fulfill the owner's instructions. Telegram messages sent to the assistant are processed to understand those instructions.

How information is used

Google data is used to answer the owner's requests and perform requested file operations. Relevant file content or excerpts may be processed by the AI service configured for the assistant to generate a response. The assistant does not use Google data for advertising or sell it.

Storage and sharing

Google OAuth credentials are stored encrypted on the owner's Mac mini. The assistant may retain conversation and operation history locally as part of its normal operation. Requests and responses may pass through Telegram and the configured AI service. Google data is not shared with unrelated third parties for their own use.

Control and deletion

The owner can stop future Google access by revoking the application's connection in Google Account connections. To request deletion of locally retained assistant data, contact the operator at pakvd88@gmail.com. Revoking Google access does not automatically erase previously retained local conversation history.

Changes

This policy may change when the assistant's configuration changes. The current version will be posted on this page.
